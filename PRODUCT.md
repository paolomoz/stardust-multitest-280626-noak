# PRODUCT — Starbucks (target, brand-faithful modernized)

_provenance: stardust:direct, Mode A (brand-faithful, signal-strong). Strategy
inherited from stardust/current/PRODUCT.md; surface modernized, brand DNA pinned._

## Register
**brand** — photography-led marketing/commerce site for a global coffee retailer.

## Users
Coffee customers browsing the menu; Rewards members & prospects; gift-card buyers;
people finding stores / ordering ahead or delivery; visitors researching the company.

## Product Purpose
Showcase the Starbucks menu and seasonal offers, drive Rewards sign-ups, sell &
send gift cards, route to ordering and the store locator, and present the brand
(heritage, coffee craft, partners, social impact). One-line: *make it a great day
for coffee, and make the next order one tap away.*

## Brand Personality
Warm, premium-but-approachable, optimistic, community-minded; coffee-craft pride
with seasonal playfulness. Signature Siren mark, deep House Green, generous
lifestyle photography, clean white space.

## Anti-references
No 2019-SaaS centered-hero-with-double-CTA silhouette; no dense data tables; no
corporate-stiff tone; no generic stock clip-art; no all-uppercase body shouting.

## Design Principles
1. Photography leads — full-bleed lifestyle imagery and real product shots carry pages.
2. House Green + white + foam tint; restrained, accessible accent palette.
3. Pill CTAs, rounded product cards, the circular Siren as the persistent mark.
4. Clear IA — Menu / Rewards / Gift / Stores / About, self-contained nav.
5. SoDoSans throughout; mixed-case headlines, uppercase reserved for eyebrows/short CTAs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: House Green on white/foam only where contrast passes; alt text on every
real image; semantic heading order; reduced-motion honored.
