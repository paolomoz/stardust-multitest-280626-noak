# PRODUCT — Xfinity (target, faithful redesign)

## Register
**brand** — consumer marketing/commerce for a national telecom. Conversion-led, deal-forward.

## Users
US residential consumers shopping or managing home Internet, Mobile, TV & Streaming, Home Phone, and Home Security/Smart Home. Secondary: existing customers paying bills, moving, finding stores, checking deals.

## Product Purpose
Sell and bundle Xfinity (Comcast) residential connectivity and entertainment, drive plan-builder/shop conversions, surface limited-time deals, and route existing customers to account tools. One-line: _"The easiest way to get connected — fast internet, mobile, TV and streaming, your way."_

## Brand Personality
Friendly, optimistic, value-forward, mainstream-modern. Confident benefit headlines, concrete prices, reassurance. Approachable, not premium-exclusive. Xfinity purple as the signature spark against a clean near-black/white system.

## Anti-references
- Enterprise/B2B coldness, austere luxury minimalism, dense data dashboards.
- **Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette** (centered hero + dual gradient CTA + three-up feature icons): explicitly disallowed — modernize within Xfinity's own visual language.
- No fabricated offers/prices, no logo-as-editorial, no stock-photo filler.

## Design Principles
1. **Lead with benefit + price** — every card states the value and the number.
2. **Friendly geometry** — rounded 16px cards, pill CTAs, generous imagery.
3. **Purple as the spark** — reserve Xfinity purple for accents, CTAs, and key emphasis on a near-black/white ground.
4. **Scannable modules** — offer/feature tiles that read fast across audiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Purple #5a23b9 on white ≈ 7:1 (passes). Reserve fine-print as caption text (not headings). One H1 per page, proper heading hierarchy, alt text on imagery.
