# PRODUCT — PayPal (target, redesign)

> Target strategy for the faithful-modernized redesign migrated to AEM Edge
> Delivery. Mode A (brand-faithful): palette and type pinned to the captured
> PayPal brand surface; IA preserved; execution modernized.

## Register
**brand** — multi-audience marketing site for a global payments product
(consumer + business + enterprise tracks, plus help & resource content).

## Users
- **Consumers** — pay, send, split, save, Pay Later, crypto, cards, rewards.
- **Businesses / merchants** — accept payments, invoicing, POS, checkout.
- **Enterprise / partners** — large-scale payments platform & partnerships.
- **Existing customers** — help center + business resource center readers.

## Product Purpose
Move, manage, and protect money for consumers and businesses — one trusted
wallet and brand for checkout, transfers, savings, credit, crypto, and
merchant acceptance.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, modern, confident, warm-but-financial. Big bold display type,
PayPal navy + bright blue + sky accents, generous whitespace, real lifestyle
photography, layered product cards. Authoritative on security and compliance.
Traits: `product`, `transactional`, `civic-formal` (trust), `modular-catalogue`.

## Anti-references
- Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual CTA in flat primary blue).
- Stock-photo soup; abstract gradient scenery in place of real product imagery.
- Cluttered fintech dashboards; low-trust dark patterns.
- Logo-as-editorial; fabricated stats or testimonials.

## Design Principles
1. **Trust first** — clarity, security cues, legal precision, AA contrast.
2. **Bold benefit headlines** — large display type carries the value prop.
3. **Real imagery** — photography + layered cards, never abstract filler.
4. **Pill CTAs, high contrast** — one primary action per surface.
5. **One coherent system** — navy/blue/sky unifies consumer + business + enterprise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA: navy/white and blue/white CTAs pass AA; reserve sky #60CDFF for
large text / accents only (fails AA as small body on white). Semantic headings,
alt text on every image, reduced-motion honored.
