# PRODUCT — Sony Group Portal (target)

> Faithful redesign, modernized. Brand-faithful (Mode A): palette and type pinned to the captured Sony surface; IA preserved; execution modernized.

## Register
**brand** — corporate marketing/editorial portal, image-forward, low form-density, multi-audience.

## Users
- Investors & analysts (IR, reports, stock)
- Job seekers (Careers)
- Press & media (News Releases)
- Sustainability / ESG stakeholders
- Partners and the general public exploring Sony's businesses
- Design & technology enthusiasts

## Product Purpose
The global corporate portal for Sony Group Corporation — "fill the world with emotion, through the power of creativity and technology." It communicates purpose, businesses, technology, sustainability, design, careers, and investor relations, and routes a broad set of audiences to the right destination. It is not a storefront.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, confident, editorial, premium. Near-monochrome (black wordmark on white), generous whitespace, large brand imagery, quiet chrome. Photography carries emotion; typography stays neutral and unobtrusive. Editorial, slow-paced, publication-register.

## Anti-references
- Loud, saturated, gradient-heavy consumer-electronics marketing.
- Generic 2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-color CTAs).
- Dense dashboard/tool UI.
- Cluttered link-farm corporate homepage.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, decorative side-stripes (impeccable hard rules).

## Design Principles
1. **Let imagery lead** — full-bleed photography and clean card grids carry every page; chrome recedes.
2. **Monochrome restraint** — black/white/grey base; color arrives through photography and a single restrained blue accent.
3. **Editorial whitespace** — sections breathe (balanced density); content is grouped and scannable.
4. **Wordmark as anchor** — the Sony logo is the one persistent brand mark, in every header and footer.
5. **Preserve the signature** — the home hero auto-rotates through brand stories; reproduce the motion, never flatten it (with a reduced-motion still fallback).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Text over photography always carries a legibility scrim. Mixed-case headings. `prefers-reduced-motion` halts the carousel. All images carry alt text.
