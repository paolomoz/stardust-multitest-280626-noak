# Sycamore Partners — Target Strategy

## Register
**brand** — institutional marketing site for a private equity firm. No app surface.

## Users
- Limited partners / institutional investors performing diligence on the firm.
- Management teams and intermediaries at prospective portfolio companies.
- Press, recruiting candidates, and portfolio-company stakeholders.

## Product Purpose
Present Sycamore Partners as a leading private equity firm specializing in consumer,
distribution, and retail-related investments (~$11B aggregate committed capital,
30+ investments). Convey strategy, the portfolio of recognizable consumer brands,
current news, and the team — with institutional credibility and restraint.

## Brand Personality
Institutional, understated, confident, established. Serif-led editorial typography
(Merriweather) against a clean sans (Work Sans). A single sycamore-green accent and
the sycamore-tree mark signal growth, longevity, and stewardship. The brand opens
with a quiet animated tree reveal, not a loud marketing splash. Traits:
`civic-formal`, `institutional`, `editorial`, `restrained`.

## Anti-references
- Loud, gradient-heavy SaaS/startup aesthetics.
- The Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-blue CTAs).
- Stock-photo "business handshake" filler; logo-as-editorial.
- Forced timed splash/redirect (the current site's entry pattern — to be removed).
- jQuery/Bootstrap framework chrome and `loader.gif` placeholders.

## Design Principles
1. **Restraint over spectacle** — whitespace, serif headings, one green accent.
2. **Let the portfolio talk** — recognizable consumer/retail brands carry trust.
3. **Editorial credibility** — clean stat bands, real numbers, sourced disclaimers.
4. **Preserve the signature** — reproduce the sycamore-tree motion as an inline,
   non-blocking, reduced-motion-aware hero; never flatten it to a still or block
   content behind a timed redirect.
5. **Quiet motion** — the `arrival` motion register: gentle entrances, no spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Inherit impeccable defaults. Hold WCAG AA: reserve sage `#78915c` for large text
and accents; keep body links/small UI on `#4f752d` or deeper. Legibility scrim on
any text over the video hero.
