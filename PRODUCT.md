# Paramount — Product / Strategy (target)

## Register
**brand** — corporate identity site for Paramount (Paramount Skydance Corporation):
who we are, our brands, our businesses, leadership, news, press, and careers.

## Users
- **Talent / job seekers** — careers is a first-class pillar (benefits, life-at-
  Paramount, internships, talent-pipeline programs, streaming/technology jobs).
- **Press & media** — news + press-release feeds.
- **Investors** — handed off to ir.paramount.com.
- **Partners, fans, general public** — brand-portfolio and business storytelling.

## Product Purpose
Present Paramount as one unified global entertainment company spanning streaming,
studios, broadcast, cable, and sports — communicating scale, heritage (since 1912),
and a forward-looking, streaming-era voice; driving recruitment, press, and
investor relations.

## Brand Personality
Bold · cinematic · confident · iconic · institutional-yet-playful. Anchored by the
Paramount mountain + stars emblem and a deep-navy / electric-blue identity with
vivid accent pops (yellow, magenta, cyan) echoing the diversity of the brands.
Display typography is a signature (large, all-caps statements).
Traits: `display-typography-signature`, `signage-led`, `civic-formal`,
`institutional`.

## Anti-references
- Not a streaming product UI (no player / catalog browsing — that is paramountplus.com).
- Not a dense investor portal (links out to ir.paramount.com).
- Avoid the Generic-2026-SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary-blue CTA +
  stock photography). Avoid all-caps body copy (shout fatigue).

## Design Principles
1. **Lead with the moving image.** Reproduce the signature film-montage motion in
   hero surfaces; never flatten it to a still. (axis: expressive → committed)
2. **Mark-led portfolio.** Show the brand marks in a scannable grid, not prose.
   (axis: density → balanced, ia-fidelity → reimagined)
3. **Cinematic contrast.** Deep-navy canvas, high-contrast white type, a fixed
   scrim under every hero overlay. (axis: distinctiveness → distinctive)
4. **Mixed-case voice.** ALL-CAPS for display/eyebrow/CTA only; mixed-case for
   body and descriptive headings. (anti-cliché)
5. **Accent the range.** Use yellow/magenta/cyan as category markers to surface
   the portfolio's diversity without diluting the navy identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Scrim-guaranteed contrast on all hero overlays; `prefers-reduced-
motion` static fallback for the film-montage hero; semantic heading hierarchy.
