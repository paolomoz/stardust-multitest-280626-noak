# PRODUCT — Samsung US (target)

## Register
**brand** — consumer-electronics marketing + merchandising. Product storytelling that drives
consideration and routes to purchase and support.

## Users
- Shoppers researching Galaxy phones, tablets, watches, TVs, monitors, and Bespoke home
  appliances.
- Existing owners needing support, manuals, repair status, and registration.
- Deal-seekers tracking offers, trade-in, and Samsung Rewards.

## Product Purpose
Showcase Samsung's full US consumer lineup with confident, AI-forward storytelling; surface
limited-time offers; and route cleanly to buy and support. One canonical action verb per
surface ("Shop"), with secondary "Learn more" text links.

## Brand Personality
Premium, clean, innovation-forward, quietly confident. Big product photography on a calm
black-and-white canvas; Samsung Blue as a deliberate signature, not decoration. Modernized:
mixed-case headlines, tighter type, more breathing room, and a more decisive single CTA per
section than the source's stacked-CTA merchandising.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered spec-sheet retailer pages and discount-bin promo design.
- The 2019 SaaS silhouette (centered hero + dual primary CTAs).
- Heavy gradients, glassmorphism, neon. Logo-as-editorial.
- Stacked redundant CTAs (source home shows many competing "Shop/Buy/Pre-order" verbs).

## Design Principles
1. **Photography leads.** Full-bleed key visuals carry the message; chrome stays out of the way.
2. **Monochrome canvas, blue with intent.** Black/white/grey ground; Samsung Blue reserved for
   primary action and brand signature.
3. **One decisive CTA per section.** Replace stacked merchandising CTAs with a single primary
   action plus an optional text link.
4. **Modular, scannable rails.** Category pages are clean stacks of hero → grid → offer →
   comparison → editorial cross-links.
5. **Consistent global chrome.** One mega-nav header and one footer, identical site-wide.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Legibility scrim on any text over photography. Reduced-motion honored. Real alt
text on product imagery.
