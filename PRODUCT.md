# PRODUCT — Bank of America (target, modernized faithful redesign)

## Register
**brand** — consumer + small-business banking marketing surface in front of authenticated Online Banking. Migration target preserves the existing IA and content verbatim; only the execution is modernized.

## Users
- Consumers shopping checking/savings, credit cards, auto loans, mortgages, home equity.
- Existing customers seeking servicing, help/FAQ, and the mobile app.
- Small-business owners (distinct nav pillar).
- Wealth / Merrill prospects (cross-sell pillar).

## Product Purpose
Help prospects discover and apply for banking products and educate them on financial decisions, while routing existing customers to servicing and the app. Brand promise: _"What would you like the power to do?"_ Purpose: _"help make financial lives better through the power of every connection."_

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, established, reassuring, accessible, civic. Security and FDIC insurance are always present. Imagery is photographic and aspirational. Voice is plain, benefit-led, compliance-aware.
Personality traits (for motion register selection): `civic-formal`, `institutional`.

## Anti-references
- Not playful / startup-y; no edgy or experimental voice.
- Not minimalist-luxury; mass-market and offer-driven, but calmer than the source.
- Avoid the **Generic-2026-SaaS** silhouette (centered hero + dual gradient CTA): the bank reads as institutional, not SaaS.
- No auto-advancing offer carousel (source weakness #1).

## Design Principles
1. **Trust first** — FDIC / Equal Housing / security messaging is deliberate and always present.
2. **One ask per section** — single primary CTA per band; no competing donor-verb-style CTA sprawl.
3. **Calm card discovery** — one consistent image-led card system for product entry points.
4. **Compliance in plain sight** — rates/fees/footnotes accompany claims, set as quiet secondary type.
5. **Recognizably Bank of America** — flagscape navy/red, pill buttons, soft cards preserved.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Maintain AA contrast (reserve flagscape red and grey for non-text/meta where they fail AA on tinted surfaces). Preserve skip links, alt text, and the accessible-banking program entry point. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored.
