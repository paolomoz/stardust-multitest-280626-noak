# Improvements — en (home)

1. **[dated-pattern]** The portal hero is a small-stage rotating banner with thin chrome and a cramped headline; the captured slides are 1390×780 brand films (Spider-Man, BRAVIA, Legacy of Sound) that deserve full-bleed editorial scale.
   *Fix:* Render the carousel full-bleed with a left-anchored headline over a legibility scrim; keep the auto-rotation as the signature motion.

2. **[ia-clutter]** The home page exposes ~100 links (full mega-nav duplicated inline), fragmenting attention.
   *Fix:* Lead with the hero, a tight "explore Sony" card grid of the 6 primary sections, and a Latest News rail; push the exhaustive link set into the footer.

3. **[contrast]** Hero headline text sits directly over bright photography with no scrim in the source.
   *Fix:* Add a bottom-left gradient scrim so AA contrast holds on every slide.

4. **[cliché]** Dense 11–14px corporate type at a flat scale gives weak hierarchy.
   *Fix:* Adopt a 1.25 modular scale with mixed-case Inter headings; clear h1/h2/h3 steps.

5. **[missed-opportunity]** Latest News is a plain text list on the source home.
   *Fix:* Promote to image+date+headline cards that reuse captured article imagery.
