# PRODUCT — Bank of America (current state, descriptive)

_Descriptive snapshot of the existing site. Not authored intent._

## Register
**brand** — marketing/landing surface for a consumer + small-business bank. Reads as a large-enterprise marketing site (product discovery, offers, education) sitting in front of authenticated banking (Online Banking, sign-in flows are out of scope / disallowed in robots).

## Users
- Consumers shopping for checking/savings, credit cards, auto loans, mortgages, home equity.
- Existing customers seeking servicing/help/FAQ and the mobile app.
- Small-business owners (separate nav pillar).
- Wealth / Merrill and Private Bank prospects (cross-sell pillar).

## Product Purpose
Help prospects discover and apply for banking products and educate them on financial decisions ("Better Money Habits"), while routing existing customers to servicing and the app. Brand promise: _"What would you like the power to do?"_ — purpose: _"help make financial lives better through the power of every connection."_

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, established, reassuring, accessible, civic. Heavy emphasis on security, FDIC insurance, and rewards (Preferred Rewards program). Imagery is photographic and aspirational (people, lifestyle, cards). Tone is plain, benefit-led, compliance-aware (rates, disclosures, footnotes).

## Anti-references
- Not playful / startup-y. No edgy or experimental voice.
- Not minimalist-luxury; it is dense, offer-driven, mass-market.
- Avoids jargon-heavy fintech tone; favors plain-English benefit copy.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. Trust first — FDIC / Equal Housing Lender / security messaging is always present.
2. Offer-led mastheads — rotating credit-card and deposit offers headline the home page.
3. Card-grid product discovery — image-led cards with "Learn more" / "Apply now".
4. Compliance-aware — rates, fees, and legal footnotes accompany product claims.
5. Accessible — explicit accessible-banking program, skip links, alt text.
