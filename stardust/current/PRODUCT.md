# PRODUCT — Xfinity (current state, descriptive)

_Descriptive snapshot of https://www.xfinity.com/ as extracted 2026-06-28. Not authored intent._

## Register
**brand** — consumer marketing/commerce site for a national telecom. Landing-led, deal-driven, conversion-oriented.

## Users
US residential consumers shopping for or managing home Internet, Mobile, TV/Streaming, Home Phone, and Home Security/Smart Home services. Secondary: existing customers paying bills, moving, checking deals, finding stores. _provenance: inferred from nav + copy._

## Product Purpose
Sell and bundle Xfinity (Comcast) residential connectivity and entertainment services, drive plan-builder / shop conversions, surface limited-time deals, and route existing customers to account/billing tools.

## Brand Personality
Friendly, optimistic, value-forward, mainstream. Big benefit headlines ("Get online in minutes with same-day WiFi", "Cut your mobile bill in half"), concrete price callouts, reassurance ("device protection for life"). Modern consumer-tech tone — approachable, not premium-exclusive.

## Brand Surface (observed)
- **Color:** near-black ink (#141417) on white/light-grey (#f6f6f9) base, with Xfinity purple (#5A23B9) and a brighter violet (#6138F5) as the brand accents; lavender (#BDA7E3) tints.
- **Type:** XSans for display/headlines, DM Sans for body, XfinityBrown as a rare legacy accent.
- **Logo:** lowercase "xfinity" wordmark (single-path monochrome SVG).
- **Motifs:** rounded cards (16px), pill CTAs, portrait hover-image product tiles, large price callouts, icon-led benefit rows.
- **Motion:** static heroes (no background video/canvas/Lottie detected on home); hover-reveal imagery on tiles.

## Anti-references
Avoid: enterprise/B2B coldness, luxury minimalism, dense data-tool dashboards. Xfinity is warm mass-market retail, not austere.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. Lead with the benefit and the price.
2. Big imagery, rounded friendly shapes.
3. Purple as the signature accent against a near-black/white system.
4. Modular offer tiles that scan fast.

## Information Architecture
Primary pillars: Internet · Mobile · TV & Streaming · Home Security/Home Solutions · Home Phone · Deals · Build your plan. Content families: `/learn/*` (product marketing, ~172 pages), `/hub/*` (editorial hubs, ~140), `/local/*` (locality, ~31), plus utility/legal (`/accessibility`, `/bill-pay`, `/apps`, `/compare`, `/membership`, `/site-map`).
