# PRODUCT — Sony Group Portal (current state, descriptive)

> _provenance: extracted from https://www.sony.com/en/ (15 pages, Playwright headed-Chrome). Descriptive snapshot of the existing site, not authored intent._

## Register
**brand** — corporate marketing/editorial portal. Image-forward, editorial, low form-density.

## Product Purpose
The global corporate portal for Sony Group Corporation. It is not a storefront — it communicates the group's purpose ("filling the world with emotion, through the power of creativity and technology"), its businesses (electronics, entertainment, games, music, pictures, financial services, semiconductors), corporate information, technology R&D, sustainability, design philosophy, careers, and investor relations. It routes diverse audiences to the right sub-site or document.

## Users
- Investors & analysts (IR section, reports, stock info)
- Job seekers (Careers)
- Press & media (News Releases)
- Partners, customers, and the general public exploring Sony's businesses
- Sustainability / ESG stakeholders
- Design and technology enthusiasts

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, confident, editorial, premium. Near-monochrome (black wordmark on white), generous whitespace, large brand imagery, restrained typography. Lets product/brand photography carry emotion; chrome stays quiet. Corporate but creative.

## Anti-references
- Loud, saturated, gradient-heavy consumer-electronics marketing
- Dense dashboard/tool UI
- Cluttered link-farm corporate homepages

## Design Principles (observed)
1. Image-forward — full-bleed hero imagery and card grids carry the page.
2. Monochrome restraint — black/white/grey base; color only from photography and rare accents.
3. Editorial whitespace — sections breathe; content is grouped and scannable.
4. Wordmark as anchor — the Sony logo is the single persistent brand mark.
5. Signature motion — the home hero auto-rotates through brand stories (a slideshow), a subtle dynamic touch on an otherwise still design.

## Information Architecture (templates)
- **home** — portal landing: rotating hero carousel, latest news rail, social.
- **section-landing** — products, technology, sustainability, design, corporate info, careers, IR: h1 + intro + card grids of sub-topics.
- **listing** — News Releases (dated chronological list), Corporate Blog.
- **article** — press release / "Message from the CEO": h1 + structured prose.
- **editorial/gallery** — Design Stories, Brand: image-rich story grids.
