# PRODUCT — PayPal (current-state, descriptive)

> Descriptive snapshot of https://www.paypal.com (US). Not authored intent.
> _provenance: extracted from 8 live archetype pages (playwright, 2026-06-28)._

## Register
**brand** — marketing/landing site for a global payments product. Heavy on
benefit-led headlines, product photography, pill CTAs, and trust/legal copy.

## Users
- **Consumers** — people who pay, send money, split costs, save, use Pay Later,
  crypto, debit/credit cards, and rewards (the Personal track).
- **Businesses / merchants** — accept payments, invoicing, POS, checkout
  integration, mass payments (the Business track).
- **Enterprise / partners** — large-scale payment platform + partnerships.
- **Help-seekers** — existing customers in the help center (cshelp) and
  business resource center (brc).

## Product Purpose
Let consumers and businesses move, manage, and protect money — checkout,
peer-to-peer transfer, savings, credit, crypto, and merchant payment
acceptance — under one trusted wallet and brand.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, modern, confident, friendly-but-financial. Big bold display type,
PayPal navy + bright blue + sky accents, generous whitespace, real lifestyle
photography, layered product cards. Authoritative on security and compliance.

## Information Architecture (templates observed)
- **home** — consumer brand-moment hero + benefit sections + app CTA.
- **consumer product landing** (`/us/digital-wallet*`) — feature pages.
- **business landing** (`/us/business*`) — merchant feature/marketing pages.
- **enterprise landing** (`/us/enterprise*`).
- **money-hub** (`/us/money-hub*`) — consumer money content hub/listing.
- **info/fees** (`/us/webapps/mpp/*`) — fee tables and policy/info pages.
- **security** (`/us/security*`) — trust & protection landing.
- **editorial article** (`/us/brc/article/*`) — business resource center posts.
- **help article** (`/us/cshelp/article/*`) — support Q&A pages.
- **legal** (`/us/legalhub/*`) — dense legal text (thin template).

## Anti-references
Cluttered fintech dashboards; low-trust dark patterns; stock-photo soup.

## Design Principles
1. Trust first — clarity, security cues, legal precision.
2. Bold, benefit-led headlines over feature lists.
3. Real photography + layered cards, not abstract scenery.
4. Pill CTAs, high contrast, accessible.
5. One coherent navy/blue/sky system across consumer + business.
