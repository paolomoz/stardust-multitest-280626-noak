# PRODUCT — Starbucks.com (current state, descriptive)

_provenance: extracted from https://www.starbucks.com/ (23 pages, Playwright crawl 2026-06-28)

## Register
**brand** — marketing/commerce site for a global coffee retailer. Image-led,
emotive, product-forward; not a tool/dashboard.

## Users
- Consumers browsing the menu (drinks, food, at-home coffee).
- Rewards members and prospective members.
- Gift-card buyers.
- People locating stores / ordering ahead or delivery.
- Visitors researching the company (About Us, careers, social impact, contact).

## Product Purpose
Showcase the Starbucks menu and seasonal offers, drive Rewards sign-ups, sell and
send gift cards, route customers to ordering/store-locator, and present the company
brand (heritage, coffee craft, partners, social/environmental commitments).

## Brand Personality
Warm, premium-but-approachable, optimistic, community-minded. Coffee-craft pride
with seasonal playfulness ("It's Starbucks summer", "Good energy in every sip").
Signature Siren mark; deep house green; clean white space; generous photography.

## Anti-references
No dense data tables, no corporate-stiff tone, no discount-bin clutter. Imagery is
aspirational lifestyle, never stock-clip generic.

## Design Principles
1. Photography leads — full-bleed lifestyle imagery and product shots carry pages.
2. House green + white + foam tint; restrained accent palette.
3. Pill CTAs, rounded product cards, circular Siren badge.
4. Clear IA: Menu / Rewards / Gift / Stores / About.
5. SoDoSans throughout for a consistent brand voice in type.
