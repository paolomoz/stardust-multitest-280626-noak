# Sycamore Partners — Product/Brand Strategy (current state)

## Register
**brand** — an institutional marketing site for a private equity firm. No app/tool surface.

## Users
- Limited partners / institutional investors evaluating the firm.
- Management teams and intermediaries at potential portfolio companies.
- Press and recruiting candidates.
- Portfolio-company stakeholders.

## Product Purpose
Present Sycamore Partners as a leading private equity firm specializing in consumer,
distribution, and retail-related investments (~$11B aggregate committed capital,
30+ investments). Communicate strategy, showcase the portfolio, surface news, and
introduce the team.

## Brand Personality
Institutional, understated, confident, established. Serif-led editorial typography
(Merriweather) paired with a clean sans (Work Sans). Signature sycamore-tree mark
and a restrained green palette signal growth, longevity, and stewardship. The site
opens with a quiet animated brand reveal (tree) rather than a loud marketing splash.

## Anti-references
- Loud, gradient-heavy startup/SaaS aesthetics.
- Stock-photo "business handshake" filler.
- Dense data-dashboard chrome.

## Design Principles
1. Restraint over spectacle — whitespace, serif headings, a single green accent.
2. Let the portfolio (recognizable consumer/retail brands) do the talking.
3. Editorial credibility: clean stat bands, real numbers, sourced disclaimers.
4. The tree motif and the green accent are the only ornamentation needed.

## Information Architecture (current)
- Home (`/home`) — hero tagline, stat band, overview, latest news, portfolio logo grid.
- Approach (`/approach`) — investment strategy + focus sectors.
- Team (`/team`) — investment professionals (60+ named).
- Investments (`/investments`) — portfolio logo grid → per-company detail.
- Investment detail (`/investment-info/<company>`) — logo, description, sector, link.
- News (`/news`) — chronological press releases.
- News article (`/news-article/<slug>`) — single release.
- Contact (`/contact`) — NY office details.
- Terms / Privacy & Policy Notice (`/terms-privacy-policy-notice`) — legal.
