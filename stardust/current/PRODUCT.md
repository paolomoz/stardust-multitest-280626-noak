# Paramount — Product / Strategy (current-state, descriptive)

## Register
**brand** — a corporate marketing/identity site for Paramount (Paramount Skydance
Corporation). Reads as a global media conglomerate's brand showcase: who we are,
our brands, our businesses, our leadership, news, press, and careers. Not a product
app or commerce surface.

## Users
- **Talent / job seekers** — careers is a first-class nav pillar (benefits, life-at-
  Paramount, internships, talent-pipeline programs, streaming/technology jobs).
- **Press & media** — dedicated press-release and news feeds.
- **Investors** — links out to ir.paramount.com (quarterly results, SEC filings).
- **Partners & general public / fans** — brand portfolio storytelling (Paramount+,
  Pictures, CBS, Pluto TV, MTV, Nickelodeon, BET, Comedy Central, Showtime, etc.).

## Product Purpose
Present Paramount as one unified global entertainment company spanning streaming,
studios, broadcast, cable, and sports. Communicate scale ("We are… SHAPE THE
FUTURE"), the breadth of the brand portfolio, the business segments, leadership,
and an ongoing news/press cadence; drive recruitment and investor/press relations.

## Brand Personality
Bold, cinematic, confident, iconic. Anchored by the Paramount mountain + stars
emblem and a deep-navy / electric-blue identity with vivid accent pops (yellow,
magenta, cyan) echoing the diversity of its brands. Heritage ("since 1912") fused
with a forward-looking, streaming-era voice.

## Anti-references
- Not a streaming product UI (that is paramountplus.com) — no player, no content
  catalog browsing.
- Not a dense investor-relations portal (that is ir.paramount.com) — links out.
- Avoid generic SaaS/marketing-template feel; the brand is entertainment, not B2B.

## Design Principles (observed)
1. Big, declarative typographic statements (all-caps display headlines).
2. Imagery-led: show titles, talent, and brand marks carry the page; signature
   film-montage video on brand/about surfaces.
3. Deep navy canvas with high-contrast white type and saturated accent color.
4. Clear IA pillars in a persistent header: About, News, Careers, Investors.
