# Sycamore Partners — Visual System (current state, descriptive)

## Color
- **Sycamore green `#4f752d`** is the single signature accent (rules, links, the tree mark, stat emphasis).
- **Sage `#78915c`** appears as a lighter green tint.
- Text in near-black `#333` / warm charcoal `#4f5047`; backgrounds white and off-white `#fafafa`.
- Deep navy `#041e42` appears as a rare secondary accent.

## Typography
- **Merriweather** (serif) for display/headings — editorial, institutional.
- **Work Sans** (sans) for body and UI.
- Modular scale is ad-hoc (Bootstrap-derived).

## Motifs
- **Sycamore tree** silhouette (logo and brand reveal).
- Stat band of big numbers + small caps labels.
- Portfolio logo grid.
- Restrained radii and shadows; institutional whitespace.

## Signature motion
- Full-bleed **autoplay video** brand reveal on the entry splash
  (`Sycamore-Partners-Animation.mp4`) — must be preserved in any redesign,
  with a static poster fallback and reduced-motion handling.
