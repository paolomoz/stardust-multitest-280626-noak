# DESIGN — Bank of America (current state, descriptive)

## Palette
Navy `#012169` anchors the brand (logo, dark sections, headings). Action blue `#0053C2` is the dominant link/CTA color. Flagscape red `#E31837` is the accent (logo, alerts, highlights). Neutrals: black/`#646464` text on white, with warm `#F7F4F4` and cool `#F3F6FF` section surfaces.

## Typography
Headings in **Connections** (BoA's proprietary humanist sans); body in **Roboto**. Both fall back to Arial/Helvetica. Ad-hoc scale (14/16/18/20/24/32/36/48). Weights 300/400/500/700.

## Motifs
Pill-shaped buttons (22-24px radius). Rounded cards (12-16px) with soft shadow `rgba(0,0,0,0.12) 0 2px 20px`. Image-led product cards. Persistent FDIC-insured trust strip. Offer-carousel masthead. No signature motion (static mastheads; no hero video/canvas/Lottie).

## System components
Global header (utility nav + 4-pillar primary nav + flagscape logo + search + Sign in). Footer (multi-column links + FDIC/Equal Housing disclosures + social + copyright). Mobile-app promo. Trust strip.
