# DESIGN — Sony Group Portal (current state, descriptive)

> _provenance: aggregated computed styles across 15 extracted pages + brand surface._

## Palette
Near-monochrome. Black (#000) wordmark and primary; white (#fff) backgrounds; light grey (#efefef/#f8f8f8) surfaces; mid grey (#656565) muted text. Color enters almost exclusively through photography. Rare functional accents: corporate blue (#2d75c7 / deep #01498e, used in IR/corporate contexts) and an alert red (#b83744).

## Typography
Sony's proprietary **SST** typeface (W20 Roman / Bold / Regular) with an Arial / Helvetica Neue fallback stack. Two weights in practice (regular 400, bold 700). Dense corporate sizing (≈11–16px body) with larger display headings. Humanist-grotesque character — neutral, legible, unobtrusive.

## Motifs
- Full-bleed image hero; on home an **auto-rotating carousel** (signature motion).
- Card grids: image + short label, used across section-landing pages.
- Dated news lists (month-grouped) for press releases.
- Story/gallery grids for Design and Brand.
- Minimal radii, subtle shadows only on lifted cards. Quiet chrome.

## Layout
Editorial, whitespace-rich, image-forward. Mega-menu global header; large multi-column corporate footer with a legal row.
