PARAMOUNT CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER DENNIS CINELLI TO PARTICIPATE IN MOFFETTNATHANSON'S MEDIA, INTERNET & COMMUNICATIONS CONFERENCE

LOS ANGELES and NEW YORK, May 7, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Paramount Skydance Corporation (Nasdaq: PSKY) today announced that Chief Financial Officer Dennis Cinelli will participate in the MoffettNathanson Media, Internet and Communications Conference on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, at 1:30 p.m. ET / 10:30 a.m. PT.

A live audio webcast will be available on Paramount's Investors homepage at ir.paramount.com. A replay of the audio webcast will be available in the Events and Webcasts section of the site shortly after the presentation.

About Paramount, a Skydance Corporation Paramount, a Skydance Corporation (Nasdaq: PSKY) is a leading, next‑generation global media and entertainment company, comprised of three business segments: Studios, Direct-to-Consumer, and TV Media. The Company's portfolio unites legendary brands, including Paramount Pictures, Paramount Television, CBS, CBS News, CBS Sports, Nickelodeon, MTV, BET, Comedy Central, SHOWTIME®, Paramount+, Pluto TV, Skydance Animation, Film, Television, and Interactive/Games, and the newly established Paramount Sports Entertainment. For more information, please visit www.paramount.com.
