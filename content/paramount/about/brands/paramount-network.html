Paramount Network

Paramount Network features outstanding scripted and unscripted originals, hit acquisitions and blockbuster movies - edgy, compelling content built on captivating characters with mass appeal, all available on-air, online and on-the-go. "Yellowstone," cable’s #1 summer series of 2019, starring Kevin Costner, continues to win over viewers and critics alike and will return for its third season this summer. And coming January 15th, from Executive Producers Brian Grazer and Ron Howard, and Showrunner Roberto Benabib, the gritty, contemporary, military series, “68 Whiskey,” followed by hit-maker Darren Star’s latest series, “Emily in Paris."

Unscripted hits “Ink Master” and “Bar Rescue” continue to deliver, “Ink Master: Grudge Match” means fans of tattoo artistry can celebrate Tattoosdays year round, while Jon Taffer kicks off the 7th season of “Bar Rescue” this spring. And “Wife Swap,” which scored big with viewers in its first season on Paramount Network, returns in February. And it wouldn’t be Paramount Network without hit movies, we feature the best of the best, from timeless classics like “Forrest Gump” to contemporary hits like “John Wick,” plus whole franchises, including the “Rocky” series, “Indiana Jones” and “Jurassic Park.”
