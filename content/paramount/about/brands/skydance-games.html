Skydance Games

Skydance Games delivers blockbuster gaming experiences of all kinds and is known for high-quality visuals and rich narratives. Encompassing each of the company’s two gaming studios—one led by Dan Prigg (The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners, Skydance’s BEHEMOTH) and the other led by Amy Hennig & Julian Beak (Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra, Untitled Lucasfilm Game)—as well as its games publishing, interactive licensing and transmedia storytelling teams, Skydance Games brings to life one-of-a-kind worlds and crafts compelling stories that players want to revisit over and over again. The studio’s portfolio holds award-winning titles across console, VR, PC, and mobile, including the recently released VR title Skydance’s BEHEMOTH as well as The Walking Dead: Saints & Sinners and Archangel; and the upcoming Marvel 1943: Rise of Hydra and a collaboration with Lucasfilm Games set in the legendary Star Wars galaxy.
