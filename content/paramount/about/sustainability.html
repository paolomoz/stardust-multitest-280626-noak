Sustainability

The media and entertainment industry is one of the most powerful forces influencing and reflecting culture, social views, and societal outcomes. When we talk about sustainability at Paramount, we talk about all that goes into making movies, TV shows, events, and consumer products. From the energy-efficiency measures we're taking at our studios to the rigging and protocols that keep talent safe on a set, and to the culture that enables and encourages employees to do their best work, we believe the way we create content is just as important as the images and dialogue that appear on the screen.

As Paramount's future continues to evolve, we remain focused on managing our most significant impacts and reporting on our progress with transparency and accountability.
