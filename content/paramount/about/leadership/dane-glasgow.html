Title
Dane Glasgow | Paramount
Description
Paramount is one of the world’s leading producers of premium entertainment content that connects billions of people in nearly every country in the world.
Leadership

Dane Glasgow

Dane Glasgow is the Chief Product Officer for Paramount, a Skydance Corporation. He leads the Company's long-term product vision and strategy, in addition to driving innovation across digital platforms, immersive storytelling, advertising, and AI-powered capabilities.

Dane is a seasoned senior technology executive and serial entrepreneur. Prior to Paramount, Dane was the VP of product management for Facebook where he led the product teams delivering Feed, Stories, Reels, Groups, Marketplace, Gaming, Ads and more to over 3 billion monthly active users around the world. He joined Meta in 2021 and previously led Entertainment for Facebook. Prior to Meta, Dane has held senior leadership positions in product, design, engineering and general management at Google, eBay and Microsoft. He has also launched successful businesses in entertainment, finance and communications.

Dane attended Cornell University, where he majored in computer science before leaving at the beginning of his senior year to focus his energies on launching a start-up.
