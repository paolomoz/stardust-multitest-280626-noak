Paramount Business

Studios

Paramount studios produce popular content across theatrical, broadcast, cable and streaming.

Our studios include: Paramount Pictures, an iconic producer of films since 1912 that has a library of films that includes Titanic, Forrest Gump and The Godfather, as well as franchises such as Mission Impossible and Transformers; CBS Studios, which produces global franchises like the Star Trek universe, as well as late night and daytime talk shows; and SHOWTIME/MTV Entertainment Studios, a producer of television series and films, which also includes MTV Documentary Films division.

SHOWTIME/MTV Entertainment Studios SHOWTIME/MTV Entertainment Studios produces 120+ series annually, including some of today’s biggest hits such as Yellowstone, Emily in Paris, 1923, 1883, South Park, Tulsa King, The Daily Show, RuPaul’s Drag Race, The Challenge and Jersey Shore, as well as award-winning documentaries through its acclaimed MTV Documentary Films.
