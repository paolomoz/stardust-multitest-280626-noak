Life at Paramount

Employees play a key role in shaping their experience at Paramount and in their team. Some of the ways we offer support include:

Goal-setting & check-ins: Through goal-setting and performance check-ins, employees identify their most important contributions to their team’s objectives and the company’s mission and strategy. Regular check-ins with managers help employees review progress, discuss challenges, and celebrate accomplishments. Check-ins are also a chance to check in on well-being, discuss growth and development, and receive coaching and feedback.

Global Experience Survey: Our survey measures several key aspects of our employees’ experience, including engagement and well-being, to continuously strengthen our culture and build a great place to work.

At Paramount, we know that developing new skills and building a rewarding and meaningful career is important.

Learning & Development: Paramount’s Learning & Development team offers various immersive experiences, and digital resources for everyone -from those just starting their careers to executive leaders. Our learning and development tools empower you to own your career, elevate your impact, and accelerate your career growth all while connecting with colleagues globally.

Mentoring Programs: Employees across all brands and functions can build partnerships for personal and professional growth. Our mentoring initiatives include a Buddy Program, 6-month Mentoring+, and Pop-Up Mentoring programs.

Tuition Assistance: Eligible employees can be reimbursed up to $10,000 per calendar year for undergraduate/graduate degrees, certifications and certificates.

Our range of events promote meaningful connections among our employees—from mentorship programs, to monthly town halls, to annual initiatives.
