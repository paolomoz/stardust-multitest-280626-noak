Benefits

401(k) plan with a company match for eligible employees starting on their first date of employment.

Financial Wellness Planning: Services provided by EY at no cost to employees.

Employee Assistance Fund: Receive assistance with the costs of emergency evacuation, disaster recovery, or unexpected events such as a sudden illness, car accident, or loss of a partner.

Tuition Assistance: Reimbursement up to $10,000 per calendar year for undergraduate/graduate degrees, certifications, and certificates that are aligned to current or future job roles.

College Coach Program: Help your dependents choose the best college savings plan, navigate college admissions, and manage existing education debt.

Employee Matching Gift Program: Paramount will match charitable donations to $1,000/year — doubling your impact.
