INTERNSHIPS & EARLY CAREER ROLES

Paramount is an expansive company with something for every skillset. Some of our departments include: Business Development, Communications/PR, Finance/Accounting, Human Resources, Legal, Marketing, Production, Research, Advertising and Technology.

In addition to hands-on experience, our programs include networking, mentorship, speaker sessions, career development workshops, visibility to top industry executives, and more.

Among the leaders and talent who’ve met with our interns include CBS News’ Gayle King and Nate Burleson, host of MTV’s Catfish Nev’ Schulman, and Wild 'n Out’s Justina Valentine.

Whatever your goals and passions are, we’ve got just the place for you to put your skills and smarts to work.

"Being part of a team of engineers who are constantly advancing the world of entertainment was an exciting experience! I took a deep dive into the developing world of cloud production, learning how television broadcasts can be created and streamed virtually anywhere, any place, at any time. My highlights were having the opportunity to work with the CBS Sports Golazo Network over the cloud, and gaining hands-on experience through working the Buffalo Bills NFL Sunday games!"

Focus on goal setting, elevating your resume, actioning on feedback and building your brand.

Hear from Executives at Paramount and learn more about the media/entertainment industry.

Every intern is aligned to an informal mentor to help them navigate their summer at Paramount.
